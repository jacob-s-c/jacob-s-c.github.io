Hey, I'm Jacob.

I'm a sophomore at Stanford on the pre-med track, interested in human and planetary health and health AI research.

Both come from the same hope: improving health outcomes for everyone in such an uncertain and rapidly changing chapter in humanity's history.

Outside of research

I volunteer in hospice care at the VA with United Students for Veterans' Health (USVH).

With Stanford Climate and Health (SCH), I host clinical workshops on climate change, nutrition and air pollution for underserved communities in the area, and lead climate and health education initiatives for middle and high school students in the Bay Area.

For fun

I play Teamfight Tactics, poker and chess, and I'm on Stanford's club water polo team.
